You need problem solving skills to be a good cook

  1. Handles missing ingredients.A chef can find suitable substitutes without affecting the quality of the dish.
  2. Fixes cooking mistakes.If food is overcooked, under-seasoned, or not turning out as planned, a chef can adjust it.
  3. Works under pressure.During busy meal times, chefs need to make quick decisions to keep orders moving.
  4. Reduces waste.Problem-solving helps chefs use ingredients efficiently and avoid throwing away food.
  5. Keeps customers happy.A chef can quickly solve issues with special dietary requests or customer feedback to provide a great dining experience.
